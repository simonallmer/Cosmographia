# LEX-0007: LOVE

**Domain:** Psyche (The Internal Domain)
**Genus:** Integrative Connection Force

## Definition
A profound state of identification with another entity that necessitates the voluntary sacrifice of self-interest for the well-being of the 'Other.' This force manifests in four archetypal Greek forms: Eros, Philia, Storge, and Agape.

## Logical Constraints
- **Sacrifice:** Self-Interest sacrificed? Yes (If no → Transaction / Infatuation)
- **Duration:** Sustained? Yes (If no → Impulse / Passion)
- **Archetype:** Is Eros/Philia/Storge/Agape? Yes (If no → Indifference)

## Essential Components
- **Sacrifice:** The essential hallmark: The willing abandonment of the solitary self for the other.
- **Identification:** The psychological collapse where the other's well-being is felt as one's own.
- **Archetypal Forms:** The specific mode of connection: Eros (Romance), Philia (Friendship), Storge (Family), or Agape (Universal).

## Exclusions
- **Not Lust:** Lust is a biological drive for acquisition; Love is a psychological drive for integration through sacrifice.
- **Not Liking:** Liking is based on preference; Love is based on identification.
- **Not Duty:** Duty is external obligation; Love is internal compulsion.

## Architecture of Difference (Comparisons)
- **vs. Empathy:** Empathy understands the other; Love identifies *as* the other.
- **vs. Liking:** Liking is a preference; Love is a commitment transcending preference.

## Examples
- Parental Devotion (Storge)
- The Romantic Union (Eros/Philia)

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** Two separate circles (The Self and The Other) overlapping to create a third, darker space in the center (The Union). Heavy arrows point from the centers of the two circles toward the shared space, representing the gravity of integration. The border between the circles within the union is dashed, suggesting it is becoming permeable.

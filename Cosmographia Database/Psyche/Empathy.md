# LEX-0044: EMPATHY

**Domain:** Psyche (The Internal Domain)
**Genus:** Affective Identification
**Subordinate to:** [Love](lex-0007-love)

## Definition
The ability to understand and share the feelings of another. It is the bridge between the self and the other's internal state.

## Logical Constraints
- **Recognition:** Do you perceive the other's state? Yes
- **Sharing:** Do you feel a resonance? Yes
- **Boundaries:** Do you remain a separate entity? Yes (If no → Identification / Fusion)

## Essential Components
- **Cognitive Empathy:** Understanding the other's perspective.
- **Affective Empathy:** Mirroring the other's emotional response.
- **Compassion:** The resulting desire to help or connect.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Sympathy:** Sympathy is feeling *for* someone (distance); Empathy is feeling *with* someone (proximity).
- **vs. Love:** Love is the force of integration; Empathy is the sensory apparatus that enables it.

## Examples
- Feeling sad when a friend is crying.
- Understanding why a rival is frustrated.
- "Putting oneself in someone else's shoes."

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** Two overlapping wavy lines of the same frequency. Where they overlap, the color is intensified and the amplitude increases.

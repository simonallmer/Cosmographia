# LEX-0045: TRUST

**Domain:** Psyche (The Internal Domain)
**Genus:** Social Predictive Reliance
**Parent of:** [Treaty](lex-0047-treaty)

## Definition
The firm belief in the reliability, truth, ability, or strength of someone or something. It is the invisible glue of the Societas domain.

## Logical Constraints
- **Uncertainty:** Does it exist in the face of risk? Yes (If no → Certainty / Knowledge)
- **Reliance:** Do you act based on this belief? Yes
- **Reciprocity:** Is it usually mutual? Often (If no → Faith)

## Essential Components
- **Benevolence:** The belief that the other intends no harm.
- **Competence:** The belief that the other has the ability to act.
- **Consistency:** The history of past reliability.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Knowledge:** Knowledge is based on data; Trust is based on expectation.
- **vs. Faith:** Faith is belief without evidence; Trust is belief based on pattern (though risk remains).

## Examples
- Lending money to a friend.
- Relying on a bridge to hold your weight.
- Market confidence in a currency.

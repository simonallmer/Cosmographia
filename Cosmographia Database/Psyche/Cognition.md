# LEX-0023: COGNITION

**Domain:** Psyche (The Internal Domain)
**Genus:** Mental Process

## Definition
The mental action or process of acquiring knowledge and understanding through thought, experience, and the senses. It is the internal labor of the Human observer.

## Logical Constraints
- **Processing:** Does it involve manipulation of information? Yes (If no → Sensation)
- **Understanding:** Does it aim for meaning? Yes (If no → Reflex)
- **Substrate:** Is it mental? Yes (Though can be modeled in Logica)

## Essential Components
- **Perception:** The intake of data from the environment.
- **Attention:** The selective focusing on specific data streams.
- **Memory:** The storage and retrieval of processed information.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Intelligence:** Intelligence is the capacity; Cognition is the active process.
- **vs. Emotion:** Emotion is the affect; Cognition is the analysis.

## Examples
- Solving a mathematical problem.
- Recognizing a face.
- Language acquisition.

**Visual Structure:** A stylized brain profile. Inside, a series of glowing pulses moving between three distinct regions labeled P (Perception), A (Attention), and M (Memory).

# LEX-0043: BOREDOM

**Domain:** Psyche (The Internal Domain)
**Genus:** Attention Deficit State

## Definition
The state of feeling weary and restless through lack of interest in one's current activity or environment. It is the "friction" of a mind without a target.

## Logical Constraints
- **Under-stimulation:** Is there a lack of challenge? Yes
- **Restlessness:** Does it produce a desire for change? Yes
- **Duration:** Does it feel prolonged? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Low Arousal:** The lack of energetic engagement with the environment.
- **Lost Attention:** The mind's inability to find a stable anchor point.
- **Temporal Dilation:** The perception that time is moving more slowly.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Apathy:** Apathy is a lack of feeling; Boredom is a feeling of dissatisfaction with the lack of engagement.
- **vs. Leisure:** Leisure is the time of non-work; Boredom is the failure of that time to be experiential.

## Examples
- Waiting in a long line.
- A repetitive task without a clear goal.
- Being in a room with no sensory or intellectual stimulation.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A perfectly straight horizontal line across the center. A slight, shallow curve dips below the line in the middle, then returns to the flat state.

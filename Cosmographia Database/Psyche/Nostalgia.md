# LEX-0014: NOSTALGIA

**Domain:** Psyche (The Internal Domain)
**Genus:** Affective Memory State

## Definition
A sentimental longing or wistful affection for the past, typically for a period or place with happy personal associations. It is a bridge between the present self and a perceived "golden age" of experience.

## Logical Constraints
- **Temporal Direction:** Is it directed at the past? Yes (If no → Hope / Anxiety)
- **Affect:** Is it bittersweet? Yes (If no → Pure Grief / Pure Joy)
- **Object:** Is it an experience? Yes (If no → Material Hoarding)

## Essential Components
- **Memory Buffer:** The distorted or idealized recollection of past events.
- **Wistfulness:** The emotional ache for what is no longer accessible.
- **Identity:** The used of the past to stabilize the current sense of self.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Memory:** Memory is data retrieval; Nostalgia is data flavored by emotion.
- **vs. History:** History is an objective record (Chronos); Nostalgia is a subjective filter.

## Examples
- Re-watching a childhood cartoon.
- Visiting one's hometown after decades away.
- Listening to a song that defines a specific year of one's life.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A curved arrow pointing backward into a soft, glowing cloud. The arrow's tail is sharp and current, while its head blurs into a sepia-toned haze.

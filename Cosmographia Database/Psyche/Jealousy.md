# LEX-0059: JEALOUSY

**Domain:** Psyche (The Internal Domain)
**Genus:** Affective Preservation State
**Related to:** [Envy](lex-0042-envy)

## Definition
The state of being resentfully suspicious of a rival or an excessive guarding of the attentions or possessions one currently enjoys. Jealousy is the "fear of loss" in the social and emotional sphere.

## Logical Constraints
- **Possession:** Do you currently enjoy the object/attention? Yes (If no → Envy)
- **Threat:** Do you perceive a rival? Yes
- **Preservation:** Is the goal to keep what is yours? Yes

## Essential Components
- **The Enjoyed Object:** The relationship, quality, or possession currently held.
- **The Rival:** The perceived threat to that possession.
- **Suspicion:** The undue focus on the potential for loss.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Envy:** Envy is when you want something that someone else has; Jealousy is when you don't want others to have something—or someone—that you do. 
- **vs. Fear:** Fear is general; Jealousy is specific to the preservation of a social/emotional bond or status.

## Examples
- "To have and have not": Envy is the "have not," Jealousy is the "have" (and fearing loss).
- An "excessive guarding of the attentions one currently enjoys."
- "Even God, who wants all the worship to himself, gets jealous" (Exodus 20:5).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A solid central node enclosed by two thick, inward-turning hooks. A smaller node (the rival) sits outside the hooks, slightly faded.

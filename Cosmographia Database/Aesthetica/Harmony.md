# LEX-0026: HARMONY

**Domain:** Aesthetica (The Expressive Domain)
**Genus:** Vertical Auditory Relationship
**Subordinate to:** [Music](lex-0003-music)

## Definition
The vertical relationship of sounds; the combination of different musical notes played or sung simultaneously to produce chords and chord progressions. It provides the "depth" and emotional color of Music.

## Logical Constraints
- **Simultaneity:** Are multiple notes playing? Yes (If no → Melody / Monophony)
- **Relationship:** Do they interact? Yes (If no → Disconnected Noise)
- **Structure:** Is there a tonal logic? Usually Yes (If no → Atonality)

## Essential Components
- **Chords:** Three or more notes sounded together.
- **Consonance:** Sounds that are perceived as stable and restful.
- **Dissonance:** Sounds that are perceived as unstable or tense.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Melody:** Melody is horizontal (linear); Harmony is vertical (simultaneous).
- **vs. Counterpoint:** Counterpoint is the relationship between independent melodies; Harmony focuses on the resulting chords.

## Examples
- A choir singing in four parts.
- A strummed guitar chord.
- The use of a "power chord" in rock music.

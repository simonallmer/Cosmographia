# LEX-0027: MELODY

**Domain:** Aesthetica (The Expressive Domain)
**Genus:** Horizontal Auditory Sequence
**Subordinate to:** [Music](lex-0003-music)

## Definition
A sequence of single tones perceived by the listener as a single entity; the linear "narrative" or "sentence" of a musical piece.

## Logical Constraints
- **Sequence:** Is it a linear progression? Yes (If no → Harmony)
- **Unity:** Is it perceived as one thing? Yes (If no → Random Tones)
- **Variation:** Does pitch change? Usually Yes (If no → Rhythm / Monotone)

## Essential Components
- **Pitch:** The highness or lowness of a tone.
- **Interval:** The distance between two pitches.
- **Phrase:** A short musical unit, similar to a linguistic clause.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Harmony:** Melody is a single string of beads; Harmony is a stack of beads.
- **vs. Rhythm:** Melody requires pitch; Rhythm only requires time.

## Examples
- The "Happy Birthday" tune.
- A flute solo.
- The vocal part of a pop song.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A single continuous line that rises and falls in a series of steps. Dots are placed at each peak and trough of the line.

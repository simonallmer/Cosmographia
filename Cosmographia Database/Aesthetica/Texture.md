# LEX-0050: TEXTURE

**Domain:** Aesthetica (The Expressive Domain)
**Genus:** Surface Quality

## Definition
The feel, appearance, or consistency of a surface or a substance. In the lexicon, Texture is the "micro-landscape" of a concept.

## Logical Constraints
- **Granularity:** Is it composed of small variations? Yes
- **Tactility:** Does it imply a "feel"? Yes
- **Uniformity:** Is the pattern consistent across the field? Often

## Essential Components
- **The Unit:** The fundamental repeating element of the texture.
- **The Roughness:** The vertical variation (height) of the surface.
- **The Rhythm:** The frequency of the variations.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Color:** Color is spectral; Texture is structural.
- **vs. Pattern:** Pattern is macroscopic (visual); Texture is microscopic (tactile).

## Examples
- The roughness of sandpaper.
- The smoothness of silk.
- The "texture" of a orchestral piece (the layers of sound).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A $5 \times 5$ grid of dots where each dot has a slightly different radius and stroke weight, creating a "visual vibration."

# LEX-0028: RHYTHM

**Domain:** Aesthetica (The Expressive Domain)
**Genus:** Temporal Auditory Pattern
**Subordinate to:** [Music](lex-0003-music)

## Definition
The essential foundational core of Music; the structured division of time into audible patterns. It is the placement of sounds in time.

## Logical Constraints
- **Pattern:** Is there a repetition or structure? Yes (If no → Random noise)
- **Time:** Is it based on duration? Yes (If no → Static Image)
- **Substrate:** Is it audible? Yes (Though can be felt as vibration)

## Essential Components
- **Beat:** The basic unit of time in music.
- **Tempo:** The speed at which the beats occur.
- **Meter:** The grouping of beats into regular patterns (e.g., 4/4 time).

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Pulse:** A pulse is steady; Rhythm is the pattern overlaid on the pulse.
- **vs. Melody:** Rhythm can exist without pitch; Melody cannot exist without rhythm.

## Examples
- A heartbeat.
- A drum beat.
- The ticking of a clock.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A straight horizontal line with vertical tick marks spaced at regular but varying intervals (e.g., short-short-long).

# LEX-0049: CONTRAST

**Domain:** Aesthetica (The Expressive Domain)
**Genus:** Qualitative Difference

## Definition
The state of being strikingly different from something else in juxtaposition or close association. It is the primary tool of perception.

## Logical Constraints
- **Comparison:** Do two values exist? Yes
- **Distance:** Is the difference significant? Yes
- **Juxtaposition:** Are they viewed together? Yes

## Essential Components
- **The Polarities:** The two extremes (e.g., Black vs. White, Loud vs. Quiet).
- **The Boundary:** The edge where the two values meet.
- **The Intensity:** The magnitude of the difference.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Harmony:** Harmony seeks resolution; Contrast seeks distinction.
- **vs. Variety:** Variety is many different things; Contrast is the *relationship* of difference between them.

## Examples
- High-contrast black and white photography.
- The contrast between a violin and a drum.
- The contrast of heat and cold.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A large square divided diagonally. The bottom-left half is solid black; the top-right half is white.

# LEX-0003: MUSIC

**Domain:** Aesthetica (The Expressive Domain)
**Genus:** Auditory Form

## Definition
The intentional arrangement of sounds and silences in time. While Music often incorporates melody and harmony, its only essential foundational component is **Rhythm**—the structured division of time into audible patterns.

## Logical Constraints
- **Intention:** Intentional? Yes (If no → Noise / Nature)
- **Substrate:** Auditory? Yes (If no → Visual Art / Sculpture)
- **Rhythm:** Patterned Time? Yes (If no → Monotone sound / Static noise)

## Essential Components
- **Rhythm:** The essential core: The division of time into patterns. A drum beat alone is Music.
- **Melody:** A sequence of single tones perceived as a single entity; the linear 'narrative' of a piece.
- **Harmony:** The vertical relationship of sounds; the combination of different musical notes played simultaneously.

## Exclusions
- **Not Noise:** Lacks intentional organization or structural DNA.
- **Not Speech:** While audible, its primary function is rhythmic/harmonic resonance rather than purely semantic information transfer.
- **Not Nature Sounds:** Lacks the human or sentient intent required to be classified as an "art" within the lexicon.

## Architecture of Difference (Comparisons)
- **vs. Sound:** Sound is the raw Materia; Music is the organized Aesthetica.
- **vs. Mathematics:** Music is often described as mathematics made audible (Logica).

## Examples
- A Single Drum Solo (Pure Music through rhythm)
- Beethoven’s 5th Symphony (Complex integration of all components)
- A 12-bar blues progression (Standardized structural template)

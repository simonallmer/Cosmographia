# LEX-0035: RATIO

**Domain:** Logica (The Formal Domain)
**Genus:** Quantitative Relationship
**Subordinate to:** [Proportion](lex-0036-proportion)

## Definition
A relationship between two numbers indicating how many times the first number contains the second. It is the fundamental atom of comparison.

## Logical Constraints
- **Comparison:** Does it involve two units? Yes (If no → Absolute Number)
- **Order:** Does the sequence matter? Yes ($1:2$ vs $2:1$)
- **Scale:** Does it persist through multiplication? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Antecedent:** The first term in the relationship.
- **Consequent:** The second term in the relationship.
- **The Colon ($:$):** The symbol of the divide between the two.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Fraction:** A fraction is a part of a whole; a ratio can compare two separate wholes.
- **vs. Number:** A number is a state; a ratio is a relationship.

## Examples
- $2:1$ (The ratio of eyes to heads on a human).
- $16:9$ (The standard widescreen aspect ratio).
- $1:1,000,000$ (Map scale).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** Two dots of different sizes separated by a vertical divider line. A notation above reads `A | B`.

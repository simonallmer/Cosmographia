# LEX-0034: INTEGER

**Domain:** Logica (The Formal Domain)
**Genus:** Numerical Unit

## Definition
A whole number that can be positive, negative, or zero, but not a fraction or a decimal. It represents discrete, countable units.

## Logical Constraints
- **Wholeness:** Is it a full unit? Yes (If no → Fraction / Real Number)
- **Countability:** Can it be mapped to a discrete set? Yes
- **Direction:** Can it be negative? Yes (If no → Natural Number)

## Essential Components
- **Zero:** The central pivot point of the integer set.
- **Successor:** The rule that defines the next unit ($n + 1$).
- **Sign:** The indicator of direction on the number line.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Fraction:** An Integer is indivisible in its wholeness; a Fraction is a division.
- **vs. Natural Number:** Natural numbers are for counting ($0, 1, 2...$); Integers include the "debt" of negative values.

## Examples
- $-5, 0, 42$.
- The number of pages in a book.
- A floor number in a building.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A horizontal line with three solid dots placed at regular intervals. A cross rests on the central dot (neutral) with a plus on the right and a minus on the left.

# LEX-0053: CATALYST

**Domain:** Logica (The Formal Domain)
**Genus:** Process Accelerator

## Definition
A substance or factor that increases the rate of an interaction or chemical reaction without itself undergoing any permanent change. In the lexicon, a Catalyst represents a "shortcut" in a logic path.

## Logical Constraints
- **Acceleration:** Does it speed up the process? Yes (If no → Passive Element)
- **Conservation:** Is it consumed? No (If yes → Fuel / Reagent)
- **Initiation:** Does it lower the threshold? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Lowering of Activation Energy:** The reduction of the "effort" required to start a process.
- **Intermediary State:** The temporary bond formed during the peak of interaction.
- **Recovery:** The return of the catalyst to its original state after the event.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Fuel:** Fuel is destroyed to provide energy; a Catalyst is preserved to provide speed.
- **vs. Command:** A command dictates; a catalyst enables.

## Examples
- An enzyme in the human body (Biological).
- Platinum in a car's exhaust system (Materia).
- A charismatic leader in a social movement (Societas).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A path blocked by a high arch. A small triangle sits at the base of the arch, causing the path to dip and pass through a much lower gap.

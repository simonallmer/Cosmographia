# LEX-0036: PROPORTION

**Domain:** Logica (The Formal Domain)
**Genus:** Geometric Balance
**Higher order of:** [Ratio](lex-0035-ratio)

## Definition
The quality of equality between two ratios. In the lexicon, Proportion is the state of systemic harmony achieved when parts relate correctly to the whole and to each other.

## Logical Constraints
- **Equality:** Are the ratios equal? Yes (If no → Imbalance)
- **Scalability:** Does it maintain form across sizes? Yes
- **Symmetry:** Does it imply a rule? Yes

## Essential Components
- **The Extremes:** The outer terms of the proportion.
- **The Means:** The inner terms of the proportion.
- **The Golden Rule:** The principle of correct relationship.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Ratio:** A ratio is a single relationship; a proportion is an equation of two ratios.
- **vs. Harmony:** Harmony is perceptual (Aesthetica); Proportion is mathematical (Logica).

## Examples
- The Golden Ratio (A specific proportion).
- Scaling a recipe for more guests.
- The Vitruvian Man (Anatomical proportion).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** Two sets of "Ratio" cosmographs (dots and dividers) placed side-by-side with an equals sign `=` in between. The scales are identical.

# LEX-0017: THRESHOLD

**Domain:** Logica (The Foundational Domain)
**Genus:** Structural Boundary

## Definition
The point or level at which a change occurs, or where a system transitions from one state to another. In the lexicon, the Threshold is the critical limit that distinguishes two different qualities or states.

## Logical Constraints
- **State Change:** Does it mark a transition? Yes (If no → Continuity)
- **Limit:** Is it a specific value/point? Yes (If no → Gradient / Spectrum)
- **Irreversibility:** Once crossed, is the state different? Yes (If no → Fluctuation)

## Essential Components
- **Input Limit:** The amount of force, energy, or data required to trigger the change.
- **Phase Shift:** The sudden transformation of the system's behavior.
- **The Event Horizon:** The point of no return.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Boundary:** A boundary separates; a threshold triggers.
- **vs. Limit:** A limit is an end; a threshold is a beginning of a new state.

## Examples
- The boiling point of water (100°C).
- A 49-page count for a "Book".
- The tipping point in an ecosystem.

**Visual Structure:** A horizontal line. Below the line, a series of small, disorganized bubbles. Above the line, a single, large, organized cube. An arrow pierces the line from below.

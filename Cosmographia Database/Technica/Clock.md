# LEX-0039: CLOCK

**Domain:** Technica (The Artificial Domain)
**Genus:** Temporal Meter
**Subordinate to:** [Calendar](lex-0041-calendar)

## Definition
A mechanical or electrical device for measuring and indicating time. A clock is the "rhythm-maker" for the social and technical domains.

## Logical Constraints
- **Precision:** Does it count regular units? Yes
- **Indication:** Does it display the count? Yes
- **Mechanism:** Is it automated? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Oscillator:** The heartbeat of the clock (pendulum, quartz, etc.).
- **Controller:** The mechanism that counts the oscillations.
- **Display:** The interface with the Human observer (hands or digits).

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Time:** Time is the domain (Chronos); the Clock is the tool (Technica).
- **vs. Calendar:** A clock measures the microscopic (seconds/hours); a calendar measures the macroscopic (days/years).

## Examples
- A grandfather clock (Mechanical).
- An atomic clock (The standard of precision).
- The sundial (The historical ancestor).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A large circle with a central point. Two lines of different lengths radiate from the center at a 90-degree angle. A small gear icon sits at the pivot point.

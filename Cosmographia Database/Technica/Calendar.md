# LEX-0041: CALENDAR

**Domain:** Technica (The Artificial Domain)
**Genus:** Temporal System
**Higher order of:** [Decade](lex-0051-decade), [Millennium](lex-0052-millennium), [Clock](lex-0039-clock)

## Definition
A system of organizing days for social, religious, commercial, or administrative purposes. This is done by giving names to periods of time, typically days, weeks, months, and years.

## Logical Constraints
- **Cycle:** Is it based on recurring events? Yes (Usually solar/lunar)
- **Standardization:** Is it shared by a group? Yes
- **Projection:** Does it map the future and past? Yes

## Essential Components
- **The Epoch:** The starting point of the calendar's count.
- **The Period:** The subdivisions of time (months, weeks).
- **The Synchronizer:** The method of aligning the system with the Materia domain (e.g., leap years).

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Clock:** Clock measures the moment; Calendar measures the span.
- **vs. Chronos:** Chronos is the raw flow of time; a Calendar is the human grid placed upon it.

## Examples
- The Gregorian Calendar (International standard).
- The Lunar Calendar.
- The Business Calendar (Fiscal years).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A $7 \times 4$ grid of small squares. One square at the center is shaded darker than the rest. A larger rectangle encloses the grid.

# LEX-0056: KAOMOJI

**Domain:** Technica (The Artificial Domain)
**Genus:** Elaborate Upright Emoticon

## Definition
A style of emoticon originating in Japan that uses characters from various scripts, including katakana and kanji, to create complex, upright facial expressions and actions.

## Logical Constraints
- **Orientation:** Upright? Yes
- **Complexity:** Script Hybridity? Yes
- **Origin:** East Asian/Japanese? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Multi-Script Type:** Mix of Latin, Japanese, and specialized punctuation.
- **Upright Layout:** Not requiring the reader to tilt their head.
- **Environmental Context:** Often including arms or objects (e.g., `-` for arms).

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Emoticon (Western):** Kaomoji are upright; Western emoticons are sideways.
- **vs. Emoji:** Kaomoji are still composed of text characters, though more complex.

## Examples
- `¯\_(ツ)_/¯` (Shrug)
- `(╯°□°）╯︵ ┻━┻` (Table Flip)
- `(◕‿◕)`

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** An elaborate typographic construct with extended "arms" and an upright central "face".

# LEX-0024: SATELLITE

**Domain:** Technica (The Artificial Domain)
**Genus:** Orbital Apparatus

## Definition
An artificial body placed in orbit around the earth or moon or another planet in order to collect information or for communication. It represents the extension of the Human gaze into the supra-terrestrial domain.

## Logical Constraints
- **Orbit:** Does it revolve around a celestial body? Yes (If no → Spacecraft / Rover)
- **Artificiality:** Is it man-made? Yes (If no → Moon / Natural Satellite)
- **Function:** Does it transmit or record? Usually Yes (If no → Debris)

## Essential Components
- **Telemetry:** The transmission of measurements and other data.
- **Payload:** The specific tools (cameras, transponders) for its mission.
- **Orbital Mechanics:** The balance of velocity and gravity that keeps it aloft.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Moon:** The Moon is a Materia satellite; the Satellite is a Technica artifact.
- **vs. Airplane:** An airplane is bounded by the atmosphere; a satellite exists beyond it.

## Examples
- Sputnik 1 (The first artificial satellite).
- GPS Satellites (Global Positioning System).
- Hubble Space Telescope.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A large circle (Earth) with a smaller, boxy tethered icon orbiting it. A beam of light (data) connects the satellite back to a point on the larger circle.

# LEX-0038: ENGINE

**Domain:** Technica (The Artificial Domain)
**Genus:** Power Transformer

## Definition
A machine with moving parts that converts one form of energy (typically heat or chemical) into mechanical force. It is the "beating heart" of complex infrastructure.

## Logical Constraints
- **Energy Conversion:** Does it transform fuel? Yes (If no → Passive Tool)
- **Mechanical Output:** Does it create motion? Yes (If no → Battery / Storage)
- **Complexity:** Does it have cycle? Yes

## Essential Components
- **The Cylinder:** The chamber of extraction.
- **The Piston:** The moving element that captures energy.
- **The Fuel Source:** The potential energy required for operation.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Motor:** Effectively synonyms, though "Engine" often implies a heat-based conversion (Internal Combustion), while "Motor" implies electrical or hydraulic.
- **vs. Tool:** A tool extends human force; an engine generates its own.

## Examples
- The Steam Engine (The catalyst for the Industrial Epoch).
- An internal combustion engine in a car.
- A jet turbine.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A large outer box containing a smaller rectangle that moves up and down along a central axis. Three sparks or arrows point into the box.

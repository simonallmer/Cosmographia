# LEX-0037: COMPASS

**Domain:** Technica (The Artificial Domain)
**Genus:** Navigational Tool

## Definition
An instrument containing a magnetized pointer which shows the direction of magnetic north and bearings from it. It represents the orientation of the Human observer in space.

## Logical Constraints
- **Magnetism:** Based on Earth's poles? Yes
- **Directionality:** Does it provide a vector? Yes
- **Stability:** Does it return to center? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Magnetic Needle:** The sensitive element that aligns with the field.
- **Pivot:** The low-friction point of rotation.
- **Cardinal Points:** The fixed framework (N, S, E, W).

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Map:** A map is a representation of space; a Compass is an orientation in space.
- **vs. GPS:** A Compass is an active physical responder to the Earth's field; GPS is a passive receiver of satellite data.

## Examples
- A pocket hiking compass.
- A mariner's ship compass.
- The digital compass in a smartphone.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A thin vertical diamond (the needle) with the top half dark and the bottom half light. It is enclosed in a faint circle with four tick marks at the poles.

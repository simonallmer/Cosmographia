# LEX-0040: LEVER

**Domain:** Technica (The Artificial Domain)
**Genus:** Mechanical Advantage

## Definition
A simple machine consisting of a beam or rigid rod pivoted at a fixed hinge, or fulcrum. It is the primary amplifier of human force.

## Logical Constraints
- **Rigidity:** Is the beam solid? Yes
- **Pivot:** Is there a fixed point? Yes
- **Multiplication:** Does it increase output force? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Fulcrum:** The pivot point.
- **Beam:** The arm of the lever.
- **Effort vs. Load:** The two points of force interaction.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Engine:** A lever is passive (it only amplifies); an engine is active (it generates).
- **vs. Scale:** A scale uses two levers in balance; a lever is typically used for imbalance (work).

## Examples
- A seesaw.
- A crowbar.
- A pair of scissors (Double lever).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A triangle (the fulcrum) with a thick horizontal line (the beam) resting on its peak. One side of the line is significantly longer than the other.

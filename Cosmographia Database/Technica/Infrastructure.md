# LEX-0022: INFRASTRUCTURE

**Domain:** Technica (The Artificial Domain)
**Genus:** Structural Foundation

## Definition
The basic physical and organizational structures and facilities (e.g., buildings, roads, power supplies) needed for the operation of a society or enterprise. It is the hidden substrate that enables higher-level social and economic activity.

## Logical Constraints
- **Necessity:** Is it required for function? Yes (If no → Luxury / Add-on)
- **Shared Use:** Is it collective? Usually (If no → Private Tool)
- **Durability:** Is it long-lasting? Yes (If no → Consumable)

## Essential Components
- **Networks:** The connections (roads, cables, pipes) that transport energy, people, or data.
- **Nodes:** The fixed points of processing or storage (power plants, servers, reservoirs).
- **Maintenance:** The ongoing energy required to prevent Entropy from destroying the structure.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Architecture:** Architecture is the visible form; Infrastructure is the functional base.
- **vs. Technology:** Technology is the active component; Infrastructure is the passive stage.

## Examples
- The Global Internet (Digital Infrastructure).
- The Interstate Highway System.
- The Power Grid.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A grid of fine grey lines at the base. Thick colored lines (representing utility) flow through the grid, connecting small boxy nodes.

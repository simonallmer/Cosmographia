# LEX-0015: CURRENCY

**Domain:** Societas (The Social Domain)
**Genus:** Economic Medium

## Definition
A generally accepted form of money, including coins and paper notes, which is issued by a government and circulated within an economy as a medium of exchange. It is the liquid representation of value.

## Logical Constraints
- **Fungibility:** Is one unit equal to another? Yes (If no → Commodity / NFT)
- **Portability:** Is it easy to move? Yes (If no → Resource / Land)
- **Trust:** Is it backed by authority? Yes (If no → Barter Item)

## Essential Components
- **Liquidity:** The ease with which it can be converted into goods or services.
- **Store of Value:** The ability to retain purchasing power over time.
- **Unit of Account:** The standard numerical unit of measurement of market value.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Money:** Money is the abstract concept; Currency is the physical or digital token.
- **vs. Barter:** Barter is direct swap; Currency is mediated swap.

## Examples
- The US Dollar (Global Reserve Currency).
- Bitcoin (Decentralized digital currency).
- Cowrie shells (Historical currency).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A circulating flow of three arrows in a circle. Each arrow contains a different symbol ($, €, ¥). At the center of the circle is a stable, solid balance scale.

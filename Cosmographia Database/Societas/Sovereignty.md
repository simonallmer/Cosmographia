# LEX-0012: SOVEREIGNTY

**Domain:** Societas (The Social Domain)
**Genus:** Political Authority

## Definition
The supreme authority and power of a state or entity to govern itself and determine its own destiny without external interference. It is the ultimate expression of political autonomy and localized control.

## Logical Constraints
- **Authority:** Is it supreme? Yes (If no → Subsidiary / Colony)
- **Autonomy:** Is it self-governing? Yes (If no → Puppet State)
- **Recognition:** Is it acknowledged? Usually (If no → De Facto Sovereignty)

## Essential Components
- **Jurisdiction:** The defined territory or scope of authority.
- **Legitimacy:** The internal or external validation of the right to rule.
- **Monopoly on Force:** The exclusive right to use physical force within its jurisdiction.

## Exclusions
- **Not Power:** Power can be exerted without sovereignty (e.g., a gang); Sovereignty requires the formal claim to ultimate authority.
- **Not Freedom:** An individual is free; a state is sovereign.

## Examples
- A nation-state issuing its own laws.
- The Treaty of Westphalia (foundational concept of modern sovereignty).
- Personal Sovereignty (the ultimate right over one's own body).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A solid, walled circle with a star or crown at the center. Arrows pointing outward indicate the exertion of will, while thick borders repel inward-pointing arrows.

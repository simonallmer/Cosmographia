# LEX-0046: TAX

**Domain:** Societas (The Social Domain)
**Genus:** Mandatory Contribution
**Higher order of:** [Property](lex-0048-property)

## Definition
A compulsory financial charge or some other type of levy imposed upon a taxpayer by a governmental organization in order to fund government spending and various public expenditures.

## Logical Constraints
- **Compulsion:** Is it mandatory? Yes (If no → Donation)
- **Authority:** Is it imposed by a sovereign? Yes (If no → Theft / Rent)
- **Reciprocity:** Is it used for collective benefit? Ideally Yes

## Essential Components
- **The Levy:** The specific percentage or amount taken.
- **The Base:** What the tax is applied to (income, property, consumption).
- **Public Utility:** The pooling of resources for infrastructure.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Fee:** A fee is for a specific service; a Tax is for the maintenance of the system.
- **vs. Theft:** Tax is backed by the Social Contract and legal legitimacy.

## Examples
- Income Tax.
- Value Added Tax (VAT).
- Property Tax.

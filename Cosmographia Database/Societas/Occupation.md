# LEX-0008: OCCUPATION

**Domain:** Societas (The Inter-Relational Domain)
**Genus:** Social Role / System Function

## Definition
A specialized, sustained activity through which an individual contributes to the maintenance of a social system in exchange for status or resources.

## Logical Constraints
- **Sustainability:** Sustained? Yes (If no → Task)
- **Utility:** Social benefit? Yes (If no → Hobby)
- **Identity:** Internalized? Yes (If no → Labor)

## Essential Components
- **Social Interface:** The point of connection between individual skill (Technica) and collective need.
- **Exchange:** The reciprocal flow of energy for status, currency, or survival assets.
- **Specialization:** The refinement of action into a professionalized and recordable role.

## Exclusions
- **Not a Hobby:** A hobby is for internal reward; an occupation is for external exchange.
- **Not Labor:** Labor is raw energy; Occupation is the structured role that energy fills.
- **Not a Task:** A task has an end-date; an occupation is a continuous state.

## Architecture of Difference (Comparisons)
- **vs. Vocation:** An Occupation is a social role; a Vocation is a 'calling' (Psyche).
- **vs. Task:** A task is temporary; an occupation is a continuous identity.

## Examples
- Blacksmith
- Software Engineer

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A gear-like circle with teeth on the outer edge (The Role) surrounding a central solid square (The Individual). The teeth represent the points of contact where the individual's energy interfaces with the larger social machine. A single vector arrow pushes into the center from the outside (Resources/Status) while others push out from the square to the gear teeth (Output).

# LEX-0048: PROPERTY

**Domain:** Societas (The Social Domain)
**Genus:** Legal Domain
**Parent of:** [Tax](lex-0046-tax)

## Definition
The legal right of an entity or person to own, use, and dispose of a resource. It is the definition of "Mine" versus "Thine."

## Logical Constraints
- **Exclusivity:** Can the owner exclude others? Yes
- **Transferability:** Can it be sold or given? Yes
- **Recognition:** Is it backed by the social system? Yes

## Essential Components
- **The Owner:** The entity holding the right.
- **The Asset:** The resource being owned (Materia or Technica).
- **The Boundary:** The limit of the owner's jurisdiction over the asset.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Possession:** Possession is the physical act; Property is the legal right.
- **vs. Natural Resource:** A resource becomes Property once it is "enclosed" by a claim.

## Examples
- Land ownership.
- Intellectual Property (Copyright).
- Personal items (e.g., a phone).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A solid square (the asset). A series of tick marks form an outer border around the square, representing the "fence." A small key icon sits at the center.

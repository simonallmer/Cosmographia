# LEX-0009: POWER

**Domain:** Societas (The Inter-Relational Domain)
**Genus:** Agency Potentiator

## Definition
The capacity of an entity to influence, command, or determine the behavior of other entities or the state of the system itself.

## Logical Constraints
- **Effect:** Causes change? Yes (If no → Influence / Suggestion)
- **Source:** Validated? Yes (Derived from recognized resources or position)
- **Scope:** Extends beyond self? Yes (If no → Autonomy)

## Essential Components
- **Agency:** The capacity to act and exert influence on the environment.
- **Impact (Return on Interaction):** The observable distortion of the social or physical grid by the entity's will. High ROI indicates a high velocity of impact from command to execution.
- **Resistance Overcoming (Friction):** The ability to enact change despite opposing forces or systemic friction (constitutional limits, social license, etc.) within the system.

## Exclusions
- **Not Authority:** Authority is the legal right (Formal); Power is the actual capacity (Action). Authority without Agency is 'dignified' but 'inefficient'.
- **Not Strength:** Strength is physical (Materia); Power is relational (Societas).
- **Not Influence:** Influence suggests; Power determines.

## Architecture of Difference (Modes of Agency)
Power is defined by the relationship between formal status and operational agency:
- **Formal (De Jure):** Power encoded in law or position. High legal ceiling but may suffer from high systemic friction (e.g., a Constitutional Monarch).
- **Actual (De Facto):** Power derived from control of resources and efficiency of execution. Measured by high ROI (e.g., a Tech CEO/Technocrat).
- **Vested:** The intersection of legitimacy and capacity. A vested agent (like a localized Mayor) holds both the formal right to act and the practical resources to influence their surroundings significantly.

## The Friction Formula
Effective Power ($P_e$) is the resultant of formal status and operational resources against the friction of the system:
$$P_e = \frac{A \times R}{f}$$
Where:
- **A (Authority):** Formal/Legal Status.
- **R (Resources):** Resource Liquidity (Money, Labor, Infrastructure).
- **f (Friction):** Systemic resistance (Jurisdictional reach, Social License, Constitutional checks).

## Examples
- **High ROI:** A CEO mobilizing billions via a single memo.
- **High Friction:** A Monarch with immense symbolic capital but no legislative agency.
- **Vested Agent:** A Mayor with recognized legitimacy and high local impact capacity.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A large, central downward-pointing arrow (The Command). This arrow hits a floor of horizontal and vertical grid-lines (The System), creating a series of radiating ripples that distort the grid as they travel outward. The closer to the arrow, the more extreme the grid distortion. The "Friction" is visualized as the thickness or rigidity of the grid lines.

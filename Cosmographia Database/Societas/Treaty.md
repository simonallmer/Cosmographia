# LEX-0047: TREATY

**Domain:** Societas (The Social Domain)
**Genus:** Formal Agreement
**Subordinate to:** [Trust](lex-0045-trust), [Sovereignty](lex-0012-sovereignty)

## Definition
A formally concluded and ratified agreement between sovereign states or international organizations. It is the "written law" of interaction between powers.

## Logical Constraints
- **Formality:** Is it written and ratified? Yes
- **Sovereignty:** Are the parties recognized powers? Yes
- **Bindingness:** Does it create an obligation? Yes

## Essential Components
- **The Protocol:** The specific terms of the agreement.
- **The Ratification:** The formal acceptance by the parties.
- **The Sunset Clause:** The conditions under which the agreement ends.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Contract:** A contract is between individuals; a Treaty is between sovereigns.
- **vs. Alliance:** An alliance is a goal; a Treaty is the document that defines the alliance.

## Examples
- The Treaty of Versailles.
- The North Atlantic Treaty (NATO).
- A bilateral trade agreement.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** Two rectangles (parchments) side-by-side. A thick solid line connects them at the center, with a small circle (the seal) resting on the line.

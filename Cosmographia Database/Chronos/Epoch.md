# LEX-0021: EPOCH

**Domain:** Chronos (The Temporal Domain)
**Genus:** Temporal Unit

## Definition
A particular period of time in history or a person's life, typically one marked by notable events or particular characteristics. An Epoch is a "container" of time defined by a specific theme or state.

## Logical Constraints
- **Duration:** Is it prolonged? Yes (If no → Event / Moment)
- **Character:** Is it distinct? Yes (If no → Mere Span)
- **Closure:** Does it have a beginning and end? Yes (Typically marked by transitions)

## Essential Components
- **Zeitgeist:** The "spirit of the time" that defines the Epoch.
- **Markers:** The specific events that initiate or terminate the period.
- **Continuity:** The internal consistency of conditions within the span.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Era:** An Era is generally longer; an Epoch is a subdivision of an Era.
- **vs. Age:** An Age usually refers to a specific technology or material (e.g., Bronze Age).

## Examples
- The Victorian Epoch.
- The Holocene Epoch.
- The Digital Epoch.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A thick horizontal segment of a line, capped by two vertical bars. The segment is filled with a unique texture or color, distinguishing it from the lines to its left and right.

# LEX-0052: MILLENNIUM

**Domain:** Chronos (The Temporal Domain)
**Genus:** Temporal Era
**Higher order of:** [Decade](lex-0051-decade)

## Definition
A period of a thousand years, typically one calculated from a traditional date. It is the scale of civilizations.

## Logical Constraints
- **Duration:** Exactly 1,000 years? Yes
- **Scale:** Does it transcend individual human life? Yes (By ~12x)
- **Continuity:** Does it imply a long-term lineage? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Centuries:** The ten subdivisions of the millennium.
- **The Turn:** The transition from one millennium to the next (e.g., Y2K).
- **Civilizational Flow:** The rise and fall of systems within the thousand-year span.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Decade:** A decade is a life stage; a millennium is a history.
- **vs. Eon:** A millennium is human-countable history; an Eon is geological time.

## Examples
- The First Millennium (1-1000 AD).
- The transition from the 2nd to the 3rd millennium.
- The age of a very old civilization or tree.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A large square made up of 10x10 tiny dots. It appears almost as a solid texture until viewed closely.

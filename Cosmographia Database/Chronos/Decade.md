# LEX-0051: DECADE

**Domain:** Chronos (The Temporal Domain)
**Genus:** Temporal Span
**Subordinate to:** [Millennium](lex-0052-millennium)

## Definition
A period of ten years. It is the primary unit of human cultural memory.

## Logical Constraints
- **Duration:** Exactly 10 years? Yes
- **Ordinality:** Is it part of a sequence? Yes
- **Unit:** Is it a subdivision of longer eras? Yes

## Essential Components
- **The Year One:** The starting point of the span.
- **The Culmination:** The tenth year.
- **The Spirit (Zeitgeist):** The social coloring often assigned to a decade.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Year:** A Year is a cycle; a Decade is a block.
- **vs. Century:** A Century is a long era; a Decade is an "epochal unit" within it.

## Examples
- The 1920s (The Roaring Twenties).
- A child's first decade of life.
- A ten-year business plan.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** Ten vertical bars of equal height. The first and last bars are slightly thicker to define the boundaries of the span.

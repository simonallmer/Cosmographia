# LEX-0020: SYMBIOSIS

**Domain:** Materia (The Physical Domain)
**Genus:** Biological Interaction

## Definition
Any type of a close and long-term biological interaction between two different biological organisms. It represents the collaborative or parasitic "entanglement" of life forms within the ecosystem.

## Logical Constraints
- **Different Species:** Are they different? Yes (If no → Social Behavior)
- **Duration:** Is it long-term? Yes (If no → Predation / Encounter)
- **Proximity:** Are they in close contact? Yes (If no → Environmental Interaction)

## Essential Components
- **Mutualism:** Both benefit (Win-Win).
- **Commensalism:** One benefits, the other is unaffected (Win-Neutral).
- **Parasitism:** One benefits at the expense of the other (Win-Loss).

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Competition:** Competition is a struggle for resources; Symbiosis is a shared existence.
- **vs. Integration:** Integration merges entities into one; Symbiosis maintains separate identities.

## Examples
- Bees and flowers (Mutualism).
- Barnacles on a whale (Commensalism).
- Ticks on a dog (Parasitism).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** Two interlocking rings of different colors. The area of intersection is a third, unified color. Arrows cycle between the two rings.

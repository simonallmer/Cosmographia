# LEX-0031: CELL

**Domain:** Materia (The Physical Domain)
**Genus:** Biological Unit
**Higher order of:** [Molecule](lex-0030-molecule)

## Definition
The basic structural, functional, and biological unit of all known living organisms. A cell is the smallest unit of life that can replicate independently.

## Logical Constraints
- **Vitality:** Is it a unit of life? Yes (If no → Crystal / Mineral)
- **Closure:** Does it have a boundary? Yes (Membrane)
- **Reproduction:** Can it replicate? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Membrane:** The protective outer layer that regulates interaction.
- **Nucleus:** The information center containing genetic blueprints.
- **Metabolism:** The engine that converts energy into action.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Molecule:** A Cell is composed of molecules but exhibits the emergent property of "life."
- **vs. Organism:** A Cell is the unit; an Organism is a collection of cells (for multicellular life).

## Examples
- A red blood cell.
- A single-celled bacterium.
- A neuron in the human brain.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A large, irregular circle (membrane) containing a smaller solid circle (nucleus). Faint wavy lines fill the space in between.

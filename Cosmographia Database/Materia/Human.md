# LEX-0002: HUMAN

**Domain:** Materia (The Physical Domain)
**Genus:** Biological Sentient Organism

## Definition
A mammalian species characterized by bipedalism, complex symbolic language, and the capacity for abstract reasoning and self-reflection. In the lexicon, the Human is the primary observer and the architect of all other Domains.

## Logical Constraints
- **Substrate:** Biological? Yes (If no → AI / Automaton)
- **Cognition:** Symbolic Reasoning? Yes (If no → Non-Human Animal)
- **Stature:** Bipedal? Yes (Distinguishing physical trait)

## Essential Components
- **Soma:** The biological body and substrate of existence.
- **Psyche:** The internal seat of consciousness and individual experience.
- **Logos:** The capacity for reason and symbolic communication.

## Exclusions
- **Not an Artificial Intelligence:** Lacks the biological substrate and organic evolution required for life.
- **Not a Non-Human Animal:** Lacks the specific threshold of symbolic reasoning and recordable history (Chronos).
- **Not a Robot:** A mechanical vessel lacking the internal "Psyche" origin.

## Examples
- A newborn child (Potentiality of Human)
- Richard Nixon (Human as a historical node)
- A contemporary individual in the present flow of time

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A central vertical axis (spine/stature) intersected by a horizontal bar at the upper third (reach/action). This structure is enclosed within a circle (the individual unity) which is itself slightly flattened at the poles to suggest a "terrestrial" anchor. Two radiating vectors from the center represent the "gaze" of the observer.

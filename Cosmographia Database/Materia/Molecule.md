# LEX-0030: MOLECULE

**Domain:** Materia (The Physical Domain)
**Genus:** Chemical Structure
**Higher order of:** [Atom](lex-0029-atom)

## Definition
A group of two or more atoms held together by chemical bonds; the smallest fundamental unit of a chemical compound that can take part in a chemical reaction.

## Logical Constraints
- **Complexity:** Does it contain ≥ 2 atoms? Yes (If no → Atom)
- **Bonding:** Are the units connected? Yes (If no → Mixture)
- **Stability:** Is it a distinct unit? Yes

## Essential Components
- **Atomic Nodes:** The individual atoms that form the structure.
- **Bonds:** The electromagnetic forces (covalent or ionic) holding the nodes together.
- **Geometry:** The specific spatial arrangement of the nodes.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Atom:** A Molecule is an emergent structure formed by atoms.
- **vs. Compound:** A Molecule is the unit; a Compound is the material in bulk.

## Examples
- $H_2O$ (Water).
- $O_2$ (Oxygen gas).
- $C_6H_{12}O_6$ (Glucose).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** Three dots arranged in a triangle, connected by thick lines. One dot is larger than the others to suggest different elements.

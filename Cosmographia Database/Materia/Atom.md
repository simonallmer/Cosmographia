# LEX-0029: ATOM

**Domain:** Materia (The Physical Domain)
**Genus:** Fundamental Physical Unit
**Parent of:** [Molecule](lex-0030-molecule)

## Definition
The smallest constituent unit of ordinary matter that has the properties of a chemical element. Every solid, liquid, gas, and plasma is composed of neutral or ionized atoms.

## Logical Constraints
- **Indivisibility (Chemical):** Can it be divided further by chemical means? No (If yes → Molecule / Compound)
- **Substrate:** Is it physical? Yes (If no → Logic Point)
- **Charge:** Does it have an electromagnetic balance? Yes (Nucleus vs Electrons)

## Essential Components
- **Nucleus:** The dense center containing protons and neutrons.
- **Electrons:** Negatively charged particles in orbital shells.
- **Empty Space:** The vast volume between the nucleus and the shells.

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Molecule:** An Atom is the singular unit; a Molecule is a cluster of atoms.
- **vs. Subatomic Particles:** Particles are the components; the Atom is the system.

## Examples
- A single Hydrogen atom.
- The atoms in a bar of gold.
- Carbon-12 (the basis for atomic mass).

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A large central dot (nucleus) surrounded by two concentric faint circles (shells). Small solid dots are placed on the outer ring to represent electrons.

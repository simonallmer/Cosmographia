# LEX-0011: ENTROPY

**Domain:** Materia (The Physical Domain)
**Genus:** Thermodynamic Property

## Definition
The measure of disorder or randomness in a system; the thermodynamic inevitable trend towards equilibrium and decay. In the lexicon, Entropy represents the literal "fading" of information and energy over time.

## Logical Constraints
- **Directionality:** Does it increase? Yes (If no → Closed/Ideal system)
- **Order:** Is disorder increasing? Yes (If no → Crystallization / Self-Organization)
- **Substrate:** Is it physical? Yes (Though can be applied to information)

## Essential Components
- **Randomness:** The lack of pattern or predictable structure.
- **Equilibrium:** The final state of maximum entropy where no further energy exchange is possible.
- **Decay:** The process of breakdown from high-order states to low-order states.

## Exclusions
- **Not Chaos:** Chaos can have hidden order (Logica); Entropy is the loss of all order.
- **Not Information:** Information is the inverse of Entropy (Negentropy).

## Architecture of Difference
- **vs. Energy:** Energy is the capacity for work; Entropy is the measure of energy that can no longer do work.
- **vs. Time:** Entropy provides the "Arrow of Time" in physics.

## Examples
- A cup of coffee cooling down to room temperature.
- The slow erosion of a mountain.
- The heat death of the universe.

## Cosmograph Suggestion
**Visual Structure:** A dense cluster of points at the center that gradually disperses and thins out as it moves towards the edges. A fading gradient from solid to transparent.

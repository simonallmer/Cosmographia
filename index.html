A Cartography of Everything

The Mission

Cosmographia is a lexicon designed to bring structure to the human experience. In a world of shifting narratives, it provides a stable foundation—a singular point of truth where language meets architecture. By identifying the precise boundaries of every concept, Cosmographia offers a blueprint for understanding the universe in its most essential form.

The Cosmograph

The soul of this system is the Cosmograph: a unique visual signature for every idea. Rather than a static image, a Cosmograph is a geometric map of a concept’s DNA. It translates abstract ideas into clear, vector-based structures, illustrating the specific requirements—such as materiality, social value, or temporal flow—that make a concept distinct. To view a Cosmograph is to see the "skeleton" of an idea.

The Seven Domains

The lexicon is organized into seven Domains, each representing a fundamental dimension of reality:

  • Materia: The Physical Domain. The realm of atoms, energy, and the tangible world.
  • Logica: The Formal Domain. The domain of mathematics, reason, and self-evident truths.
  • Technica: The Artificial Domain. The landscape of human ingenuity, from ancient tools to digital systems.
  • Psyche: The Internal Domain. The territory of the mind, encompassing emotions, memory, and consciousness.
  • Societas: The Inter-Relational Domain. The architecture of human agreement—law, status, and collective value.
  • Aesthetica: The Expressive Domain. The study of form, harmony, and the sensory experience of beauty.
  • Chronos: The Temporal Domain. The lineage of eras and events, mapping our position in the flow of time.

Navigation

  • Bridge: Discover the hidden connections between disciplines—observing how a principle in Materia might resonate as a social force in Societas.
